# Code of conduct

Ruvelo projects are open to everyone: first-time contributors and veterans, users and maintainers. We want every issue, pull request and discussion to be a place people are glad to come back to.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. People are giving their time.
- Be respectful of different opinions, experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Accept feedback the same way.
- Help newcomers find their footing.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (it's private, and we read every report), or message [@francoisbultez](https://github.com/francoisbultez) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban people from a project temporarily or permanently.

This code applies to all Ruvelo repositories and to anyone representing the project. It's inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
